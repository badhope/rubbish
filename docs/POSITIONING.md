# Product and vocabulary

**English · [简体中文](POSITIONING.zh-CN.md)**

## In one line

**Rubbish is an absurd research community: treat an odd question as a real subject, and put the process, failures, and results on the table.**

The English category is **Absurd Research Community**.

## The five main entrances

`Home / Community / Archive / Guide / Me`

- **Community:** short posts, questions, research plans, experiment logs, failure retrospectives, team-finding, and site matters.
- **Archive:** long-form research records and creative work worth keeping, grouped into empirical research, absurd research, and creative work.
- **Guide:** how to ask, how to record, how to label fiction, and how to handle copyright and privacy.
- **Me:** bookmarks, points, notifications, and settings.
- **Home:** the brand, the promise, and two actions.

## Boards

`Research Plans / Experiment Logs / Field Notes / Failure Archive / Research Discussion / Lab Life / Team Finding / Site / Announcements`

## Copy rules

1. Start from the community. Do not explain what the product used to be, or define it by what it is not.
2. Lead with what people can do: ask, frame, record, fail, review, challenge, and team up.
3. “Absurd” describes the subject, not the marketing. Methods, materials, and process still have to be clear.
4. Keep four boundaries visible: fiction is labeled, real materials are checkable, personal privacy is protected, and copyright is respected.
5. Chinese and English are written separately. Do not mix the two inside a sentence, apart from proper nouns.

## Visual rules

- One idea per screen; everything else waits below the fold.
- The community home shows only the brand, the positioning line, and two actions.
- Use lists only where continuous browsing is the point. Index rows and asymmetric sections work better on entry pages.
- Design desktop and mobile separately. Do not shrink a desktop layout into a phone screen.
