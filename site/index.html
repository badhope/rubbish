THREE WAYS IN, ONE COMMUNITY

You do not need to be a researcher first

Research plans

One question, why it matters, and how you plan to approach it. Frame it first, then find collaborators.

Logs and retros

Experiment logs, field notes, and failure reviews. The process can be rough; the material still has to add up.

Creative work

Fiction, essays, criticism, poetry, and guides. No research template required; write it well and make the discussion worth joining.

COMMUNITY RULES

Say what you mean

  • Label fictionAbsurd studies, satire, and thought experiments must say “fiction” in the title area or at the top of the body.
  • Real material stays checkableCite sources for literature, data, and images. Invented references must be marked as invented.
  • Protect real peopleDo not name private individuals, workplaces, medical records, or chat logs. Aim satire at the pattern, not an identifiable person.
  • Copyright and takedownsStolen images, rewritten work, and unauthorized reposts are removed. Copyright and privacy reports are handled first.
